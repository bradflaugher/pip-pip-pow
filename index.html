How to play

Put every domino on the board. Each colored zone has a rule about the pips (dots) inside it — make them all true and… Pow!

  • 12 pips in the zone add up to exactly 12
  • <4 pips add up to less than 4
  • >9 pips add up to more than 9
  • = every square shows the same number
  • ≠ every square shows a different number
  • ODDevery square is odd (EVEN: all even, blank counts)
  •   plain squares: anything goes

Drag a domino over two empty squares — it turns to fit, across or down. Tap it to flip it end-for-end. Drag it off the board to put it back. A domino can straddle two zones.

Levels never run out, and there's a fresh Daily puzzle every day.
